# 1D-Coefficients - RX Encoding

This page lists the individual results for for circuits with the one-dimensional encoding $R_X$ and varying qubit counts $[3\dots 6]$.
In particular, for each configuration the results comprise of the absolute coefficient mean $\mu_c(\boldsymbol{\omega})$, the corresponding relative standard deviation $\sigma_c(\boldsymbol{\omega})$ and the covariance ($\text{Cov}(\cdot, \cdot)$). In the paper these results correspond to Figure 4, Figure 12 and Figure 13 (but for $R_X$ encoding in this case); the paper only includes the $R_X$ encoding in the summary of Figures 14 and 15, as the coefficients respond to each noise type in approximately the same way as for the $R_Y$ encoding. Shown here are the plots for all qubit numbers. The abbreviations of the ansätze and noise types are explained on the [Setup](setup.md) page.
